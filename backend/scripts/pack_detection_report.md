## Thresholds in force

| setting | value | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ERA_FACTOR` | 0.64 | historical `first_cost` runs at this share of today's catalog price |
| measured era factor | 0.6429 | median over this run's non-pack catalog-matched lines (cross-check) |
| `SIGMA` | 0.35 | log-space spread of the era ratio |
| `MIN_CONFIDENCE` | 0.7 | posterior needed for a pack/piece verdict |
| `MAX_ABS_LOG` | ln(3.0) | winning reading must be within a factor of 3 |
| `REJECT_ABS_LOG` | ln(5.0) | beyond this the catalog match is discarded |
| `REJECT_NO_OVERLAP_LOG` | ln(2.0) | stricter when the names share no words |

`pack` verdicts additionally require a whole-number `quantity`. Nothing in this report changes a price: `quantity`, `first_cost`, `landed_cost`, `retail` and `extended_total` are read-only to this script.
