# Regency Reconnaissance Summary

This sanitized reconnaissance report records reusable source-discovery findings without preserving account or run-specific details.

## Findings

- Public category and product pages were reachable during the bounded investigation.
- Product links and descriptive fields were discoverable from rendered HTML.
- Commercial fields such as price or availability could not be assumed without approved authenticated access.
- No source path should be promoted to a full integration until pagination, duplicate behavior, and a representative product sample are verified.

## Recommendation

Prefer a supplier-provided export. If portal extraction is necessary, validate a small representative sample through the shared catalog contract before expanding coverage.
